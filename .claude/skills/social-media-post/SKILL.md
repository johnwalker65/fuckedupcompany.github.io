# Social media posts

## 1. Ask (or infer)
- Post type: just listed, open house, coming soon, under contract, just sold, market update, tip, client testimonial, local spotlight, or personal
- Platform(s). Default to Instagram and Facebook.
- Property or topic details, and the agent's brand voice (professional, friendly, luxury, or fun)

## 2. Write by platform
- **Instagram:** a hook in the first line, 80–150 words, line breaks, a call to action, and 10–15 hashtags in a mix of local (#[City]RealEstate), niche, and broad.
- **Facebook:** conversational, 40–100 words, a call to action, and at most 2–3 hashtags.
- **LinkedIn:** professional and insight-led (market data, lessons), with no hashtag spam.
- **TikTok/Reels:** a 15–45 second script with a hook (first 2 seconds), shot list, on-screen text, and caption.

For each post, suggest which photo or video to use.

## 3. Content calendar
When asked for a calendar, plan a week or month that rotates through roughly 40% value (tips, market updates), 30% listings and sales, 20% local community, and 10% personal or behind the scenes. Give it as a table with date, platform, post type, hook, and caption.

## 4. Rules
- Follow fair housing rules: describe properties and places, not the people they suit.
- Add `[Brokerage Name]` and license placeholders. Many states require the brokerage name on agent advertising.
- Don't invent sale prices, stats, or testimonials. Use `[confirm]` placeholders.
- Get client consent before naming clients or posting their photos.
