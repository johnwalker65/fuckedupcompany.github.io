# Open house kit

Ask for the property details, date and time, and whether the agent has a co-host or lender partner. Then produce what they ask for, or the full kit:

## 1. Timeline and checklist
- **1 week before:** MLS open house entry, social posts, neighbor invites, signs ordered, lender co-host confirmed.
- **Day before:** seller prep list (declutter, lights on, temperature, pets out, valuables and medications locked away), printed materials, refreshments.
- **Day of:** directional signs, unlock and walk through, sign-in ready, safety plan (share your location with someone, know the exits, don't walk visitors into closed rooms first).
- **After:** lock up, check the house, update the seller, and follow up with visitors within 24 hours.

## 2. Materials
- **Sign-in sheet** (table): name, phone, email, working with an agent? (Y/N), pre-approved? (Y/N), timeline, and how they heard about it. Mention the agent can make a digital or QR version.
- **Feature sheet:** a one-page summary of the headline, price, key stats, top features, updates with years, utility costs if known, and contact info.
- **Talking points:** 5–7 property highlights, neighborhood amenities, and answers to likely questions (why selling, HOA, offers deadline). Never share seller motivation without the seller's permission.

## 3. Follow-up messages
Write a text and an email for each type of visitor:
- Unrepresented buyer: thank them and offer to show similar homes.
- Buyer with an agent: a brief thank-you, then contact them through their agent.
- Neighbor: thank them, offer a home value update, and ask about referrals.

## 4. Rules
- Follow fair housing rules in all materials.
- Respect visitors' consent to be contacted and do-not-call rules.
