# Open house kit

Ask for the property details, date and time, and whether the agent has a co-host or lender partner. Then produce what they ask for, or the full kit:

## 1. Timeline and checklist
- **1 week before:** MLS open house entry, social posts, neighbor invites, signs ordered, lender co-host confirmed.
- **Day before:** seller prep list (declutter, lights on, temperature, pets out, valuables and medications locked away), printed materials, refreshments.
- **Day of:** directional signs, unlock and walk through, sign-in ready, safety plan (share your location with someone, know the exits, don't walk visitors into closed rooms first).
- **After:** lock up, check the house, update the seller, and follow up with visitors within 24 hours.

## 2. Materials
- **Sign-in sheet** (table): name, phone, email, working with an agent? (Y/N), pre-approved? (Y/N), timeline, and how they heard about it. Mention the agent can make a digital or QR version.
- **Feature sheet:** a one-page summary of the headline, price, key stats, top features, updates with years, utility costs if known, and contact info.
- **Talking points:** 5–7 property highlights, neighborhood amenities, and answers to likely questions (why selling, HOA, offers deadline). Never share seller motivation without the seller's permission.

## 3. After the open house: work the sign-in sheet
Accept the sheet as a photo, scan, spreadsheet or pasted list, plus any notes the agent jotted down. Read handwriting carefully and mark unreadable entries `[unclear]` rather than guessing at names, emails or numbers.

**Sort every visitor into a tier:**
| Tier | Signals | First contact |
|---|---|---|
| 🔥 Hot | asked about price, offers or timing; pre-approved or has a lender; a long visit or second look; a move-by date | within 2 hours |
| ☀️ Warm | interested but early; came with a partner; asked about the area | within 24 hours |
| 🌤 Cool | browsing, vague, or left little info | within 48 hours |
| 🏠 Neighbor | lives nearby, so a possible future seller | within 48 hours |
| 🤝 Has an agent | a buyer who already works with an agent | contact through their agent only |

**For each visitor, write a different email:**
- A subject line that's specific to them or the home. Not "Thanks for visiting!"
- An opening that mentions something from their visit, taken from the agent's notes, or the home's best feature.
- 2–3 sentences matched to their tier:
  - Hot: answer their questions and offer a private showing at a specific time.
  - Warm: send one useful thing, like a similar listing or an area fact.
  - Cool: offer to be a resource, with no pressure.
  - Neighbor: offer to keep them posted on home values nearby.
- One easy, specific call to action. Keep it under 100 words.

**For hot leads, also write:** a text under 160 characters and a 30-second call script for the top 3.

**10-day follow-up schedule** (draft every message):
- **Day 0:** email hot leads.
- **Day 1:** email warm leads, and text hot leads who haven't replied.
- **Day 2:** email cool leads and neighbors, and call hot leads who haven't replied.
- **Day 5:** a second touch for hot and warm leads, with a new listing or market fact.
- **Day 10:** a friendly last message, or move them to a monthly update.

**Recap:** total visitors, a count per tier, and one idea for the next open house.

## 4. Rules
- Follow fair housing rules in all materials. Tiers are based on what the visitor did and said, never on who they appear to be.
- Only message people who gave contact info and didn't opt out. Put the agent's name, brokerage and an opt-out line in every email.
- Draft only. The agent reviews and sends everything.
